⚠ Switch-day logistics

The 5:45 AM Tolantongo pickup collides with Emporio checkout. Cleanest fix: check out of Emporio before the tour and leave your bags with their bell desk for the day. Collect them on return, taxi to Bristol (both central — short hop), check in that evening. Confirm Emporio will hold luggage.

Shoot + pack

Turquoise pools + canyon = a visual break in the edit. Waterproof: GoPro + Insta360 X5. Pack swimwear, water shoes, towel, dry clothes, waterproof pouch, sun, cash, snack. Big physical day.
